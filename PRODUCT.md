# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Pantry organizer: keep track of food, update remaining stock, restock purchases, and maintain a shopping list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing React and Bun app. Exact quantities and rough levels, locations, package dates, categories, activity, local demo storage and signed-in household sync. Preserve revision conflict protection and import/export. Do not fabricate inventory or automatically add suggestions to shopping lists.

## Brand Commitments

The user requests a complete, practical redesign using Taste and Impeccable. Previous instructions reject generic green styling, request meaningful illustrations and icons, combine stock/restock/details, and place List to the left of Manage food. Keep those interaction decisions.

## Product Principles

- Make adding and updating food quick.
- Keep current stock distinguishable from a purchase amount.
- Preserve drafts on failure and guard destructive changes.
- Keep the app usable on narrow phone screens.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing source, working app, tests and user-provided screenshot. No invented product claims.
